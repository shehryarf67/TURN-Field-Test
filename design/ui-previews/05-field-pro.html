Direction 05

Field Pro

A design for TURN as it really is today: a research prototype that a field team uses. It keeps a clean navigation view, plus a Research mode switch that layers live diagnostics over the same map. It looks precise and instrument-like, with monospaced numbers, neutral greys and an orange signal accent.

  • A "Navigate / Research" switch at the top replaces "Research details" dialogs that open three levels deep. It's one map with two levels of detail.
  • A readiness strip (steps, heading, Wi-Fi, map) shows whether tracking can start, so field testers aren't left guessing why it won't.
  • Research mode adds a heads-up panel (step count, Wi-Fi age, particle spread, confidence trend) and overlay toggles for raw PDR, Wi-Fi fixes and particles.
  • Survey becomes a table of points with status and progress meters, making it quick to see coverage on site.
