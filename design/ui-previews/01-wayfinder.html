Direction 01

Wayfinder

Familiar route-planner patterns, like Google Maps indoors. The floor plan fills the screen and is turned upright to suit portrait. A single From → To card replaces the separate "Set start" and "Choose destination" steps.

  • Start and destination sit side by side in one card, so you can't forget either one. A swap button reverses the route.
  • Long, wide floors (like C-F4) are rotated to fill a portrait screen. Room labels sit inside the rooms, so the duplicate floating chips go away.
  • Choosing a direction becomes one visual compass step with a large arrow, and the confirm button is labelled in plain words.
  • Live mode uses a turn-by-turn banner at the top and a short stats strip at the bottom. Research controls move into an overflow menu.
