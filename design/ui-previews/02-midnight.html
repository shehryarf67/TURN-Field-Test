Direction 02

Midnight

A premium dark interface with frosted-glass panels on a dark navy floor plan, inspired by Apple Maps at night. The route is drawn as a glowing gradient. The map comes first, and every control floats above it.

  • Glass panels and a floating pill-shaped tab bar make the app feel polished. The map fills the whole screen.
  • A personal greeting and quick-access tiles (Washroom, Stairs, Rooms, Saved) answer the most common needs in one tap.
  • Progress dots across the top of each sheet show where you are in the "Where are you → Where to → Face → Go" flow.
  • The direction step uses a radial dial. During live navigation, a glowing halo shows accuracy and a ring shows confidence.
