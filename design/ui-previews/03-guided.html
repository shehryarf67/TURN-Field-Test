Direction 03

Guided

One decision per screen, with large touch targets and plain language. Nobody can skip a required step, because each screen asks a single question and shows a clear "Next". Warm paper-like neutrals with indigo actions give a calm, trustworthy feel.

  • A full-screen wizard (Where are you → Where to → Face → Review) replaces the bottom-sheet juggling. Back always returns to the previous step.
  • The recommended option gets a "Best" badge, so users don't have to understand reference points or Wi-Fi fixes.
  • A review screen sums up From, To and Facing, with an Edit link on each, before tracking starts.
  • Setup becomes a checklist with completion states (Map → Paths → Survey → Test walk) instead of a list of tools.
