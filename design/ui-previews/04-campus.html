Direction 04

Campus

A destination-first design with a friendly university look. Students think "I need C-402" before "where am I?", so the app starts with search, categories and rooms. Your location is asked for once you've chosen where to go. Deep teal, with coral for actions and editorial serif headings.

  • A home page with categories (Classes, Washrooms, Labs, Stairs), plus recent and nearby rooms as large cards.
  • Each room has a detail sheet (name, side of the corridor, accessibility tags) with a single "Directions" button.
  • "Where are you?" appears inside the directions sheet as one dashed card with three compact choices, so it can't be skipped.
  • Buildings page: a campus card with floor pills. Floors you've downloaded are filled in; floors you can import have dashed outlines.
